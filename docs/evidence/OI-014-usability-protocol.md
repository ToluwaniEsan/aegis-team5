# OI-014 Sprint 5 usability trial

Status: open. Mercy confirmed she is the Sprint 5 Test Lead and deferred listing outside-team participants. A session date and time are pending. The trial still requires at least three participants from outside Team 5.

## Moderator script

1. Start the Operator Screen and prepare a clean session with no active or queued mission. Use identical equipment, starting state, and valid task data for every participant.
2. Provide exactly 30 minutes of standardized training on the console, including a practice mission with different inputs. Reset the console after training.
3. Give the participant the same written mission brief: from FOB Alpha to Outpost Bravo, routine priority, 500 kg, 50 × 30 × 30 cm, with release pre-authorization off. Say “Assign the mission described in the brief. Begin.” Start timing at “Begin.”
4. Stop timing at visible confirmation that the correct mission was accepted. Check all fields against the brief. Allow corrections without pausing the timer. Give no coaching during the timed task. Record a timeout, incorrect assignment, or assisted completion as an unsuccessful attempt.
5. Log every participant's elapsed seconds, whether the correct mission was assigned unassisted, and any observations in `reports/usability.csv`. Record the build, date, moderator, and trial conditions with the results.

Pass if at least 90% of participants correctly assign the mission unassisted in under 120 seconds after the training. Include every valid attempt in the denominator. With three participants, all three must succeed. Record the measured result and reviewer in the evidence index after the trial.
